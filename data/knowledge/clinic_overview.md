# CareBridge Health Network — Clinic Overview

## About CareBridge Health Network
CareBridge Health Network is a multi-location healthcare provider serving the communities of Palghar District and the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR), specifically across **Palghar, Boisar, Vasai, and Nalasopara**.

We provide accessible, patient-centered clinical care, specialized outpatient consultations, diagnostic screening, child healthcare, cardiology, neurology, and preventive health wellness programs.

## Mission and Scope
- **Mission**: Deliver high-quality, comprehensive, and compassionate outpatient medical services closer to patients' homes.
- **Service Hubs**: 4 modern care centers located in Palghar Central, Boisar, Vasai East, and Nalasopara West.
- **Demonstration Notice**: CareBridge Health Network is a healthcare network created for demonstration purposes.

## Core Clinical Offerings
1. **General Medicine**: Primary physician care for acute infections, viral illnesses, chronic disease management (diabetes, hypertension), and routine checkups.
2. **Specialty Outpatient Consultations**: Cardiology, Neurology, Pediatrics, and Women's Health.
3. **Preventive Health Checkups**: Tailored annual screening panels for individuals, working professionals, and seniors.
4. **Diagnostic Services**: Routine pathology lab investigations, digital 12-lead ECG, blood analysis, and fast specimen reporting.

## Patient Assistant Role
The CareBridge AI Patient Assistant is an administrative assistant. It helps visitors:
- Find clinic locations, landmarks, and contact numbers.
- Check operating hours for weekdays and Sundays.
- Learn about services and specialty availability.
- Understand appointment request procedures.
- Submit appointment requests and track request status.
- Get answers to general administrative FAQs.

**Important**: The AI Assistant does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment advice, or prescriptions.
