# CareBridge Health Network — Clinical Specialties & Services

CareBridge Health Network offers primary care, specialty medicine, preventive screenings, and routine diagnostics across its branches.

---

## 1. General Medicine
- **Description**: Comprehensive primary care for acute fevers, seasonal infections, respiratory illnesses, gastrointestinal disorders, hypertension, diabetes mellitus, and regular health assessments.
- **Available Locations**:
  - Palghar Central
  - Boisar Care Center
  - Vasai Care Center
  - Nalasopara Care Center

---

## 2. Cardiology
- **Description**: Cardiac consultations, 12-lead digital ECG evaluations, hypertension management, preventive cardiovascular risk assessments, lipid review, and post-cardiac follow-up care.
- **Available Locations**:
  - Boisar Care Center
  - Vasai Care Center

---

## 3. Neurology
- **Description**: Specialist outpatient neurology consultations for chronic migraines, tension headaches, peripheral neuropathies, vertigo/dizziness, nerve health evaluations, and neurological follow-up.
- **Available Locations**:
  - Vasai Care Center

---

## 4. Pediatrics
- **Description**: Comprehensive healthcare for infants, toddlers, children, and adolescents. Services include milestone development monitoring, growth assessments, immunization tracking, and common childhood illnesses.
- **Available Locations**:
  - Palghar Central
  - Nalasopara Care Center

---

## 5. Women's Health
- **Description**: Focused consultations for women's wellness, adolescent reproductive health, prenatal guidance, PCOS/PCOD screenings, well-woman examinations, and preventive health screenings.
- **Available Locations**:
  - Nalasopara Care Center

---

## 6. Preventive Health Checkups
- **Description**: Structured wellness packages including full-body panels, diabetic screening, lipid profiles, kidney and liver function tests, and geriatric wellness packages.
- **Available Locations**:
  - Palghar Central
  - Boisar Care Center
  - Nalasopara Care Center

---

## 7. Diagnostics & Pathology
- **Description**: Routine pathology lab investigations, complete blood counts (CBC), blood glucose, HbA1c, thyroid function tests (TFT), urine routine examination, and digital ECG.
- **Available Locations**:
  - Palghar Central
  - Boisar Care Center
  - Vasai Care Center
  - Nalasopara Care Center

---

## 8. Specialist Consultations
- **Description**: Scheduled outpatient department (OPD) visits with visiting medical specialists across Cardiology, Neurology, Internal Medicine, and Preventive Health.
- **Available Locations**:
  - Boisar Care Center
  - Vasai Care Center
