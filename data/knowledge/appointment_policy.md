# CareBridge Health Network — Appointment Policy & Procedures

## Appointment Request Workflow
Patients can submit appointment requests through the CareBridge website online form or via the AI Patient Assistant.

### Required Fields for Submission
To submit an appointment request, the patient must provide:
1. **Full Name** (Patient's legal first and last name)
2. **Phone Number** (Direct contact mobile/telephone)
3. **Email Address** (For request summary and updates)
4. **Patient Type** (New Patient or Existing Patient)
5. **Preferred Location** (Palghar Central, Boisar Care Center, Vasai Care Center, or Nalasopara Care Center)
6. **Requested Service** (Selected specialty available at the chosen location)
7. **Preferred Date** (Valid upcoming date)
8. **Preferred Time Slot** (Morning: 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM, Afternoon: 12:00 PM - 4:00 PM, Evening: 4:00 PM - 8:00 PM)

---

## Important Booking & Confirmation Rules
- **Request vs. Confirmation**: The AI assistant and website submit an **appointment request**, NOT a guaranteed instant booking.
- **Verification Requirement**: The AI assistant must never claim that an appointment is confirmed unless availability has been verified by the clinic's scheduling coordinator.
- **Mandatory Submission Notice**:
  > *"Your appointment request has been submitted. Our clinic team will contact you to confirm the appointment."*

---

## Walk-in Policy
- Walk-in visits may be accepted depending on location, time of day, and doctor availability.
- Priority is always given to patients with pre-scheduled requests.
- Patients are strongly advised to contact the preferred clinic center before arriving without an appointment.

---

## Doctor Selection
- Doctor availability is determined by the selected clinical specialty and branch schedule.
- The clinic scheduling coordinator will confirm available doctors when reviewing and confirming the appointment request.

---

## Rescheduling Policy
- Existing patients may submit a rescheduling request through the online patient tracker or by calling the clinic directly.
- Upon submitting a reschedule request, the appointment status is updated to `RESCHEDULE_REQUESTED` pending coordinator verification.

---

## Cancellation Policy
- Patients can submit a cancellation request through the online tracker or by contacting clinic support.
- The appointment status will be updated to `CANCELLED`.
