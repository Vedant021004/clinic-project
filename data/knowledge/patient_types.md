# CareBridge Health Network — Patient Categories & Onboarding

## New Patients
- **Welcome & Guidance**: First-time visitors are welcomed and guided through CareBridge's multi-location network.
- **Intake Flow**:
  1. Overview of locations across Palghar, Boisar, Vasai, and Nalasopara.
  2. Review of offered clinical departments and diagnostic capabilities.
  3. Explanation of the appointment request process.
  4. Contact details (Name, Phone, Email) collected only when the patient decides to proceed with a request.

---

## Existing Patients
- **Tailored Services**: Existing patients who have previously visited or registered with CareBridge are assisted with:
  - Submitting new appointment requests.
  - Submitting rescheduling requests for existing appointments.
  - Submitting cancellation requests.
  - Tracking appointment status using their Phone Number or Request ID (`CB-XXXXXX`).
  - Checking updated branch operating hours.

---

## Privacy & Sensitive Medical Records Guardrail
- **No Medical Records in Chat**: To protect patient privacy and comply with healthcare data standards, the AI assistant does not request, upload, or process sensitive personal medical records, lab reports, radiology imaging, or prescription renewals through the chat.
- **Doctor Consultation Direction**: Patients with questions regarding diagnoses, test interpretations, medication dosages, or treatment plans are directed to speak directly with their attending physician.
