# CareBridge Health Network — Patient Safety & Emergency Guidelines

## Non-Diagnostic Medical Scope
The CareBridge AI Patient Assistant is an administrative tool and **is NOT a medical doctor or licensed healthcare practitioner**.

The AI system is strictly programmed under the following non-negotiable boundaries:
1. **Never Diagnose**: Will not assess symptoms or determine medical conditions.
2. **Never Prescribe**: Will not recommend medications, dosages, or pharmaceutical treatments.
3. **Never Interpret**: Will not evaluate radiology scans, MRI/CT reports, or blood laboratory values.
4. **Never Alter Plans**: Will not modify ongoing clinical care or advise changing prescribed drugs.

---

## Acute Emergency Protocol
If a patient reports severe, acute, or potentially life-threatening symptoms, the system immediately suspends administrative conversation and presents the emergency protocol:
- **Red Flag Symptoms**: Severe chest pain, symptoms of heart attack or stroke, severe shortness of breath, sudden numbness or paralysis, severe uncontrolled bleeding, loss of consciousness, choking, poisoning, or severe head trauma.
- **Immediate Action**:
  - Direct the patient or companion to dial **108** (National Emergency Medical Service in India) immediately.
  - Advise seeking immediate transportation to the nearest hospital emergency casualty department.
  - Contact CareBridge Urgent Help at **+91 22 4000 1000** for location-specific emergency desk coordination.
