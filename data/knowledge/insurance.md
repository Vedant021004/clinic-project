# CareBridge Health Network — Insurance & Payment Guidelines

## Insurance & Cashless Policy
- **Coverage Variability**: Insurance coverage, Third-Party Administrator (TPA) partnerships, and cashless hospital/clinic empanelment vary by clinic location, specific clinical specialty, diagnostic procedure, and insurance provider.
- **No Online Guarantee**: The AI Patient Assistant does not guarantee or authorize insurance claims or cashless eligibility.
- **Verification Process**: Patients seeking to use insurance or mediclaim policies should contact their target clinic center directly or speak with the billing front-desk team at **+91 22 4000 1000** with their policy number and TPA card prior to their appointment.

## Consultation & Procedure Pricing
- **Transparent Fee Structure**: CareBridge maintains transparent, clinic-specific fee schedules.
- **Pricing Disclosure**: Costs vary depending on consultation type (General Physician vs. Specialty Cardiologist/Neurologist) and required lab investigations.
- **Anti-Fabrication Rule**: The AI assistant and website do not invent or quote speculative prices. Our front-desk coordinator will communicate exact charges and accepted payment modes (UPI, Debit/Credit Card, Cash, Net Banking) during appointment confirmation.
